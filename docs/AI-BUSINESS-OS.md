# Team Work AI Business Operating System

## Mission

Build an adaptive AI-operated business platform that can discover opportunities, run bounded experiments, measure outcomes, change strategy when performance is weak, and shut down persistently loss-making ventures.

## Operating loop

1. Discover opportunity.
2. Create a business hypothesis.
3. Allocate a bounded validation budget.
4. Execute the minimum viable experiment.
5. Measure revenue, direct cost, operating cost, acquisition cost, conversion and customer outcomes.
6. Evaluate unit economics and margin.
7. SCALE when the business is profitable inside policy.
8. CHANGE when profitable but inefficient or when the first loss occurs.
9. SHUTDOWN when the loss limit is exceeded or repeated strategy changes fail.
10. Preserve learning and redirect resources to the next opportunity.

## AI hierarchy

AI Boss -> AI Office -> AI HR -> AI Finance -> AI Website -> AI Stock -> AI IT.

AI Boss owns portfolio-level decisions. AI Finance enforces budget and spending limits. AI IT enforces technical safety. Destructive or financially material actions require explicit approval according to configured policy.

## Non-negotiable controls

- No fabricated revenue or delivery.
- No unlimited spending.
- No automatic irreversible action without an approval gate.
- No strategy marked successful without measured evidence.
- No business remains ACTIVE merely because an agent likes the idea.
- Every strategy change creates an audit record.
- Every shutdown preserves the reason and learning.
- Emergency stop must halt outbound and financially material automation.

## Reliability target

The system must be designed for graceful failure: detect, isolate, retry safely, recover from persisted state, and report. Absolute zero-failure operation is not technically guaranteeable.
